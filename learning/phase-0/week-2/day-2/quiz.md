# Part 1 — Multiple Choice Questions

### Q1. What does voltage represent?

A. Flow of electrical charge  
B. Electrical push or potential difference  
C. Opposition to current  
D. Mechanical movement

**Answer:** B

---

### Q2. What is the SI unit of electrical current?

A. Volt  
B. Ohm  
C. Ampere  
D. Watt

**Answer:** C

---

### Q3. What is the SI unit of resistance?

A. Ampere  
B. Watt  
C. Volt  
D. Ohm

**Answer:** D

---

### Q4. If voltage remains constant and resistance increases, what happens to current?

A. It increases  
B. It decreases  
C. It becomes automatically zero  
D. It remains unchanged

**Answer:** B

---

### Q5. Which equation represents electrical power?

A. P = V × I  
B. P = V ÷ I  
C. P = I ÷ R  
D. P = R ÷ V

**Answer:** A

---

### Q6. A component operates at 5 V and draws 2 A. What is its electrical power?

A. 2.5 W  
B. 7 W  
C. 10 W  
D. 20 W

**Answer:** C

**Calculation:**

P = V × I

P = 5 × 2

P = 10 W

---

### Q7. Why can't a 7.4 V battery simply be connected directly to every RoboNova component?

A. Every component has identical voltage requirements  
B. Components can have different allowable voltage ranges  
C. Current cannot flow from a battery  
D. Motors do not require electricity

**Answer:** B

---

### Q8. Which RoboNova component generally requires substantially greater current capability than a small sensor?

A. Motor

**Answer:** A

### Q9. What can excessive resistance in a motor connection cause?

A. More current to reach the motor  
B. Less available current and potentially weaker motor performance  
C. Automatic increase in battery voltage  
D. The controller becomes a motor

**Answer:** B

---

### Q10. Which statement correctly distinguishes voltage and current?

D. Voltage produces mechanical movement while current measures resistance

**Answer:** C
