## 🧠 Key Concepts Referenced

The resources above support the following Day 3 concepts:

### Ohm's Law

\[
V = I \times R
\]

### Current

I = \frac{V}{R}

### Resistance

R = \frac{V}{I}

### Voltage

V = I \times R


### Relationships

With resistance constant:

> **Voltage ↑ → Current ↑**

With voltage constant:

> **Resistance ↑ → Current ↓**

These relationships are fundamental to understanding electrical circuits and are useful when analyzing RoboNova's future power and motor systems. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

## ⚠️ Engineering Note

Ohm's Law is very useful, but not every real-world electrical device behaves as an ideal resistor. Some components have nonlinear or changing electrical characteristics.

Therefore:

> **Use Ohm's Law together with real component datasheets, operating limits, and measurements.**

OpenStax specifically notes that the simple linear relationship described by Ohm's Law does not apply to every material or device. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
