# 🤖 RoboNova

# Phase 0 – Week 1

# Mini Project

## Project Title

RoboNova Version 1 – Concept Design

---

## Mission

RoboNova is an intelligent humanoid service robot designed to assist people with everyday household tasks. It can communicate with users, perform simple tasks such as bringing a water bottle, and provide entertainment through greetings and interactive actions. Its features can be customized for household assistance, entertainment, or basic home monitoring.

---

## Specifications

* Height: 2–3 feet
* Weight: Approximately 8–12 kg
* Movement: Legs
* Power Source: Li-ion Battery
* Communication: Mobile Application
* Connectivity: Wi-Fi and Bluetooth

---

## Hardware

### Controller

* Raspberry Pi
* Arduino Uno

### Sensors

* Camera
* Microphone
* Ultrasonic Sensor
* Touch Sensor

### Actuators

* Servo Motors
* DC Motors
* Stepper Motors

### Mechanical Body

* 3D Printed Plastic

---

## Workflow

1. The microphone receives the user's command.
2. The controller understands the command.
3. The camera identifies the target object.
4. The ultrasonic sensor detects obstacles.
5. The controller plans a safe path.
6. The actuators move RoboNova.
7. RoboNova completes the task and returns safely.

---

## Future Improvements

## Conclusion

This mini project represents the first complete conceptual design of RoboNova. It combines the knowledge gained during Week 1 into a single engineering design.
