# Part 3 – Short Answers

### 16. What is a Degree of Freedom (DOF)?

**Answer:**

A Degree of Freedom (DOF) is one independent way in which a robot or an object can move.

---

### 17. What is a robot joint?

**Answer:**

A robot joint connects two robot links and allows them to move relative to each other.

---

### 18. Explain the difference between a revolute joint and a prismatic joint.

**Answer:**

A revolute joint provides rotational movement around a fixed axis, while a prismatic joint provides linear (sliding) movement along one axis.

---

### 19. Why are robot links compared to human bones?

**Answer:**

Robot links are similar to human bones because they provide a rigid structure and are connected by joints, allowing the robot to move.

---

### 20. RoboNova needs to pick up a bottle from a table. Which joints and how many DOFs should its arm have?

**Answer:**

RoboNova should use revolute joints at the shoulder, elbow and wrist. Fixed joints can be used where movement is not required. A basic arm should have approximately 3–4 DOFs so it can reach, bend and position its hand to pick up a bottle.
