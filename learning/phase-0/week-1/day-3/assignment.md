## Task 3

### Why Does RoboNova Belong to Multiple Robot Categories?

RoboNova belongs to multiple robot categories because it has different characteristics. It is a **service robot** because it helps humans with household tasks. It is a **humanoid robot** because it is planned to have a human-like body. It is a **mobile robot** because it can move from one place to another. It is also planned to become an **autonomous robot** by making decisions and performing tasks with little or no human intervention.

---

## Task 4

### Compare Humanoid and Autonomous Robots

A **humanoid robot** is classified by its human-like body structure, including features such as a head, torso, arms, and legs. An **autonomous robot** is classified by how it operates. It can sense its environment, make decisions, and perform tasks with little or no human intervention. Humanoid describes the robot's appearance, while autonomous describes its behavior.

---

## Task 5

### What Type of Robot Will RoboNova Become?

After two years of development, RoboNova is expected to become an intelligent personal assistant robot. It will help people by performing household tasks and interacting naturally with them. RoboNova will have a human-like body, making it a humanoid robot. It will move independently around its environment as a mobile robot. It will also make decisions using artificial intelligence, allowing it to operate as an autonomous robot. Overall, RoboNova will combine multiple robot categories to become a smart and helpful home assistant.
