# 🤖 RoboNova

# Phase 0 – Week 1 – Day 5

# Assignment

## Task

Design RoboNova's actuator system by selecting the most suitable actuator for each major robot component.

Include:

* Head
* Camera
* Shoulder
* Elbow
* Wrist
* Wheels
* Future Extension Mechanism

Explain why each actuator was selected.

---

# RoboNova Actuator Design

## 1. Head

**Actuator:** Servo Motor

**Reason:**

The head needs controlled left and right rotation for natural movement and interaction.

---

## 2. Camera

**Actuator:** Servo Motor

**Reason:**

The camera must rotate to specific angles for object detection and tracking.

---

## 3. Shoulder

**Actuator:** Servo Motor

**Reason:**

The shoulder requires precise rotational movement to position the robotic arm accurately.

---

## 4. Elbow

**Actuator:** Servo Motor

**Reason:**

The elbow bends to fixed angles, making a servo motor the best choice for accurate control.

---

## 5. Wrist

**Actuator:** Servo Motor

**Reason:**

The wrist needs precise positioning for gripping and manipulating objects.

---

## 6. Wheels

**Actuator:** DC Motor

**Reason:**

The wheels require continuous rotation for forward, backward, and turning movements. DC motors provide good speed, torque, and cost efficiency.

---

## 7. Future Extension Mechanism

**Actuator:** Linear Actuator

**Reason:**

If RoboNova later includes an extendable arm or lifting mechanism, a linear actuator will provide smooth and accurate straight-line movement.

---

# Future Improvements

* Add stepper motors for high-precision mechanisms if RoboNova performs tasks requiring extremely accurate positioning.
* Upgrade servo motors to higher-torque models when lifting heavier objects.
* Select actuators based on payload, speed, precision, power consumption, and budget.

---

# Final Design Summary

| Robot Part       | Selected Actuator | Reason                  |
| ---------------- | ----------------- | ----------------------- |
| Head             | Servo Motor       | Precise rotation        |
| Camera           | Servo Motor       | Accurate viewing angles |
| Shoulder         | Servo Motor       | Controlled arm movement |
| Elbow            | Servo Motor       | Fixed-angle rotation    |
| Wrist            | Servo Motor       | Precise positioning     |
| Wheels           | DC Motor          | Continuous rotation     |
| Future Extension | Linear Actuator   | Straight-line movement  |

---

# Reflection

Through this assignment, I learned that different robot parts require different actuators depending on the type of movement they perform. Servo motors are ideal for precise angle control, DC motors are best for continuous wheel movement, stepper motors are suitable for high-precision applications, and linear actuators provide straight-line motion. Selecting the correct actuator is an important engineering decision that directly affects RoboNova's performance, efficiency, and future capabilities.
