# Part 3 – Short Answers

### 16. What is an actuator?

**Answer:**

An actuator is a device that converts electrical, hydraulic, or pneumatic energy into mechanical movement after receiving commands from the controller. It is called the muscle of a robot.

---

### 17. Why are actuators compared to human muscles?

**Answer:**

They are compared to human muscles because both create movement. Human muscles move the body after receiving signals from the brain, while actuators move robot parts after receiving commands from the controller.

---

### 18. What is the difference between a servo motor and a DC motor?

**Answer:**

A servo motor provides precise fixed-angle rotation and can hold a specific position. A DC motor provides continuous rotation and does not know or hold a fixed position by itself.

---

### 19. Why is a servo motor the best choice for RoboNova's elbow?

**Answer:**

A servo motor is the best choice because the elbow needs to bend to precise angles. A DC motor rotates continuously, making it unsuitable for accurate elbow positioning.

---

### 20. Imagine RoboNova needs to move around the house, pick up a cup, and extend its arm to reach a high shelf. Which actuator would you choose for each task?

**Answer:**

* **Wheels:** DC Motor — provides continuous rotation for movement.
* **Elbow:** Servo Motor — provides precise fixed-angle movement.
* **Extendable Arm:** Linear Actuator — provides straight-line extension and retraction.
