## Lessons Learned

* Sensors allow RoboNova to perceive its environment.
* Every sensor has a specific responsibility.
* The camera provides vision and object recognition.
* The microphone detects sound and voice commands.
* The ultrasonic sensor measures distance and detects obstacles.
* The touch sensor detects physical contact.
* The controller combines information from multiple sensors and makes intelligent decisions.
* Actuators convert controller commands into mechanical movement.
* Robots follow the Sense → Think → Act cycle.
